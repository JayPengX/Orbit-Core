隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 14 日

  • Orbit 不賣資料、沒有廣告、沒有第三方追蹤碼。
  • 你存在 App 裡的東西（課表、記帳、追蹤、進度）只用來在你的裝置之間同步。
  • 使用統計是匿名的次數：哪些功能被打開、快不快、有沒有出錯。不知道你是誰，隨時可以關掉。
  • 要下載或刪除你的資料，寫信到 privacy@jaypengx.com，系統會自動處理。

1. 我們是誰

Orbit 是一組免費的 App（運動、交通、天氣、課表、記帳、新聞、單字、節拍、法律與模擬證券），位在 orbit.jaypengx.com，由個人開發者 JayPengX（下稱「我們」）在台灣經營，是你個人資料的蒐集者與管理者。本政策依中華民國《個人資料保護法》撰寫，也照顧到其他地區常見的隱私權要求。

2. 我們蒐集什麼

2.1 你的 Orbit Pass 帳戶

  • 通行碼：只存經過加密雜湊的結果，我們看不到原本的通行碼。
  • 通行密鑰：只存公開金鑰和你取的名稱，指紋或臉孔資料永遠留在你的裝置上。
  • 你在各 App 存的資料：設定、追蹤的球隊與路線、課表與待辦、記帳的每一筆、單字進度、節拍作品、法律收藏、模擬投資的帳戶，以及正在進行的事（行程、YouBike 租借、專注計時）。
  • 救援信箱（你自己加入時才有）：用來找回帳戶和處理資料請求，驗證過才會存。
  • 登入憑證：每台裝置的登入憑證（會過期），讓你不用每次輸入通行碼。

2.2 匿名使用統計

為了知道哪些功能有用、哪裡太慢、哪裡壞掉，App 在你離開畫面時送出一小份次數：

  • 打開了哪個 App、哪些分頁，以及載入花了多久；
  • 錯誤訊息（數字、網址、引號裡的文字都先去掉）；
  • 登入、註冊、設定等步驟走到哪一步；
  • 裝置種類（手機、平板、電腦）、作業系統、語言、深色或淺色、是否已登入、從主畫面或瀏覽器打開；
  • 今天、這週、這個月是不是第一次打開（只是「是／否」）。

統計不含帳號、裝置代碼、IP 位址、位置，也不含你輸入或存的任何內容。伺服器只把它們加總成每天的數字，無法分辨是誰。你可以在 Orbit Pass › 隱私 › 使用統計關掉；瀏覽器開啟「全球隱私控制」（GPC）時也會自動不傳。

2.3 只在你用到某個功能時

  • 位置：天氣和交通在你允許後，用你的位置算當地天氣、附近站牌和路線，不會存下來。你開了天氣提醒時，會存下約 10 公尺精度的位置，直到你關掉那則提醒。
  • 照片：記帳的「拍照記帳」和課表的照片匯入，照片會交給 AI 讀出文字，讀完不保存。
  • 你打給 AI 的文字：問 Orbit、問法律等智慧功能，你的問題會交給 AI 公司產生回答（見第 4 節）。我們只記錄次數和用量，不記錄對話內容。
  • 搜尋：地點搜尋的文字會交給 Google 地點服務。
  • 錄音：節拍的錄音只在你的裝置上處理，永遠不會上傳。
  • 通知：你打開通知時，會存下瀏覽器的推播位址和你要的提醒（比賽、天氣、公車等）。
  • 除錯紀錄：只有你在 Orbit Pass 按下送出時才會傳給我們，內容是 App 的錯誤和裝置資訊，用來修 bug。

2.4 技術資料

  • IP 位址：只在伺服器記憶體裡短暫計數，用來防止濫用（例如每小時的登入次數），不寫入資料庫。主機商 Cloudflare 依其政策處理連線資料。
  • 裝置儲存：App 用瀏覽器的儲存空間保存你的資料副本和設定，讓 App 離線也能打開。登出時全部清除。我們不使用廣告或追蹤 cookie。

3. 為什麼用這些資料

  • 提供你要的服務：登入、同步、提醒、AI 回答。
  • 讓 Orbit 更好：找出常用和沒人用的功能、慢的地方和錯誤。
  • 維持安全：防止濫用、偵測錯誤、在故障後還原資料。

我們不賣、不出租你的資料，不拿來打廣告，也不做行銷用的個人檔案。

6. 你的權利，以及怎麼行使

依個人資料保護法，你可以查詢、閱覽、取得副本、補充或更正、要求停止蒐集處理利用，以及要求刪除你的個人資料。

  • 更正：你存的東西都可以直接在 App 裡修改或刪除。
  • 停止使用統計：Orbit Pass › 隱私 › 使用統計，關掉就不再傳送。
  • 下載副本、刪除帳戶、停止統計：寫信到 privacy@jaypengx.com（主旨寫「下載」、「刪除」或「停止統計」都可以）。系統會自動回信，附上確認連結：
    • 從你帳戶驗證過的救援信箱寄信，點連結就能完成；
    • 其他信箱寄的信，點連結後要輸入你的通行碼證明是你本人。
  • 確認連結 24 小時內有效。下載副本每個帳戶 30 天一次；刪除帳戶隨時都可以，而且無法復原。

這些請求全部由系統自動處理，通常幾分鐘內完成，不會超過法律規定的期限。

7. 未成年人

Orbit 適合學生使用，但不是為 13 歲以下的兒童設計。未滿 18 歲的使用者，請先讓父母或監護人看過本政策並同意後再使用。我們不會刻意蒐集兒童的個人資料；如果發現 13 歲以下的兒童建立了帳戶，家長可以用第 6 節的方式刪除。

8. 安全

所有連線都加密（HTTPS）。通行碼只存加密雜湊，登入憑證有期限，資料庫只有經營者能存取。沒有系統是百分之百安全的；如果發生影響你資料的事故，我們會依法盡快通知。

9. 本政策的變更

政策更新時，這頁的日期會改變；重大變更會在 App 裡提醒你。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

  • Orbit sells no data, shows no ads and has no third-party trackers.
  • What you keep in the apps (schedules, spending, follows, progress) is used only to sync it across your devices.
  • Usage stats are anonymous counts: which features open, how fast, and what breaks. They never say who you are, and you can turn them off.
  • To download or delete your data, email privacy@jaypengx.com; it's handled automatically.

1. Who we are

Orbit is a family of free apps (Sports, Transit, Weather, Class, Money, News, Words, Beats, Law and play-money Securities) at orbit.jaypengx.com, run from Taiwan by the independent developer JayPengX ("we"), who controls your personal data. This policy follows Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and the privacy expectations common elsewhere.

2. What we collect

2.1 Your Orbit Pass account

  • Your pass: stored only as a salted hash; we can't see the pass itself.
  • Passkeys: only the public key and the name you give it. Your fingerprint or face never leaves your device.
  • What you keep in each app: settings, teams and routes you follow, class schedules and to-dos, every money entry, word progress, beats you make, saved laws, your play-money account, and what you're in the middle of (a trip, a YouBike rental, a focus timer).
  • Recovery email (only if you add one): used to recover your account and answer data requests; stored only once verified.
  • Sign-in tokens for each device (they expire), so you don't type your pass every time.

2.2 Anonymous usage stats

To learn which features help, what's slow and what breaks, the apps send a small set of counts as you leave a screen:

  • which app and tabs were opened, and how long loading took;
  • error messages (with numbers, addresses and quoted text removed);
  • how far sign-in, sign-up and setup got;
  • the kind of device (phone, tablet, computer), operating system, language, dark or light mode, signed in or not, opened from the Home Screen or a browser;
  • whether this is the first open today, this week or this month (just yes or no).

They contain no account, device ID, IP address or location, and nothing you type or keep. The server only adds them up into daily totals and can't tell one person from another. Turn them off in Orbit Pass › Privacy › Usage stats; a browser with Global Privacy Control turned on sends none.

2.3 Only when you use a feature

  • Location: once you allow it, Weather and Transit use it for local weather, nearby stops and routes, without storing it. If you turn on a weather alert, a location accurate to about 10 m is kept until you turn that alert off.
  • Photos: Money's receipt scan and Class's photo import send the photo to an AI to read the text; it isn't kept.
  • What you ask the AI: Ask Orbit, Ask Law and other smart features send your question to an AI provider for the answer (section 4). We record counts and usage, not the conversation.
  • Searches: place searches go to Google's place service.
  • Recordings: Beats processes recordings on your device; they are never uploaded.
  • Notifications: when you turn them on, your browser's push address and the alerts you asked for (games, weather, buses) are stored.
  • Debug log: sent only when you tap Send in Orbit Pass; it holds the app's errors and device details, to fix bugs.

2.4 Technical data

  • IP addresses are counted briefly in server memory to stop abuse (such as sign-ins an hour) and are never written to a database. Our host, Cloudflare, handles connection data under its own policy.
  • Device storage: the apps keep a copy of your data and settings in your browser's storage so they open offline; signing out clears it all. We use no advertising or tracking cookies.

3. Why we use it

  • To provide what you ask for: sign-in, sync, alerts, AI answers.
  • To improve Orbit: find the features people use and don't, slow spots and errors.
  • To keep it safe: prevent abuse, detect faults, restore data after a failure.

We never sell or rent your data, use it for advertising, or build marketing profiles.

6. Your rights and how to use them

You may ask to see, get a copy of, correct or complete your personal data, ask us to stop collecting or using it, and ask us to delete it.

  • Correct: everything you keep can be changed or deleted right in the apps.
  • Stop usage stats: Orbit Pass › Privacy › Usage stats; turned off, nothing is sent.
  • Get a copy, delete your account, stop stats: email privacy@jaypengx.com (a subject like "export", "delete" or "stop stats" is enough). You get an automatic reply with a confirmation link:
    • sent from your account's verified recovery email, the link alone completes it;
    • sent from any other address, the link asks for your pass to prove it's you.
  • Links work for 24 hours. A copy can be downloaded once every 30 days per account; deleting is always possible and cannot be undone.

Every request is handled automatically, usually within minutes, and always within the time the law allows.

7. Children and teens

Orbit suits students but is not designed for children under 13. If you are under 18, please have a parent or guardian read this policy and agree before you use Orbit. We don't knowingly collect children's personal data; if a child under 13 has made an account, a parent can delete it as in section 6.

8. Security

Every connection is encrypted (HTTPS). Passes are stored only as hashes, sign-in tokens expire, and only the operator can reach the database. No system is perfectly secure; if an incident affects your data, we will tell you as the law requires.

9. Changes

When this policy changes, the date above changes; important changes are shown in the apps.
