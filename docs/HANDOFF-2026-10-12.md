## 3. Quadra Play on the proxy (the owner's redesign)

### How it works today (read before changing)
- **Settling is on the phone**: `checkResults` in `public/app.js`
  (~line 2424) runs while 紀錄 is open, every 90 s (`RESULT_CHECK_MS`) or the
  live rate; reads every started, undecided pick's source
  (`fetchOutcomes` → now the kit's `readOutcomes`), `legResult` each,
  `applyResults` (settles once all decided, or a lost parlay pick at once),
  `commitAccount`. A pick unfound 3 days after its start is void.
- **The "bet finished" notice is a guess**: `syncPush` (app.js ~2118) hands
  the Worker each open slip's expected end (start + `GAME_HOURS` by family)
  as a plain push item. The Worker doesn't know the result.
- **Money**: the pass's wallet (`eco.js`, collection `eco-wallets`) is a
  ledger of entries with fixed ids merged by union (`mergeWallet`): Play
  writes `stake-<slip>`, `payout-<slip>`, `plus-<slip>`; the pool is their
  sum. Play's own data (`odds-study-accounts`) is its account, gzip+base64
  (`lib/codec.mjs`, `gz1:`), slips with each pick's `result` and `final`.
  Play republishes its ledger from its account (`poolEntries`).
- **Push**: `push.js`. Lists per account+app in KV (`push:<account>:<app>`),
  cron every 5 min (`sendDue`), `check: { espn, event }` already polls ESPN
  until final. **Only for phones subscribed to notifications** (`record.sub`)
  — so settling must not live in the push list.

### Stages 2 and 3: built and live (second session, 2026-10-12 afternoon)

Shared-Proxy `3600ffd` (deployed; `/play` answers), Quadra Play `03fdd9e`.
What was built differs from the design below in these ways (simpler):
- **Indexed by slip, read by game.** `odds-open/<account>_<slip>` holds the
  slip; `odds-open/_index` each slip's next look (one read a run). The
  run reads every due slip's started picks together through `readOutcomes`,
  which reads each URL once, so one read of a game settles every slip on it
  (slips on one game fall due together). No game documents.
- **Its own cron:** `1-59/2 * * * *` (`SETTLE_CRON`), so it has its own 50
  fetches and CPU: 6 slips looked at, 3 settled a run at most.
- **Results applied by Play with `applyResults`** (`odds-settled/<account>`,
  GET /play, ack): partial results of a parlay too. The wallet's
  `odds:payout-<slip>` comes back into Play's ledger through
  `recoverFromWallet` first, so applying never pays again (test in
  Play's `tests/settled.test.mjs`).
- **The Worker pays only a slip whose stake is in the wallet** (a slip handed
  over but never synced is left for Play to pay itself).
- **Cash out**: `op: 'cashout'` takes the slip's document from `open` to
  `cashed` (updateTime precondition); settling takes it to `settling` the
  same way first. Settled first: Play is told 這張已經結算了 and applies it.
- **Play still reads** the open picks' games on 紀錄 for live scores and cash
  out (as before), and settles a slip itself only if the Worker hasn't 30
  hours after its games began (`BACKSTOP_MS`): money stays right through a
  Worker outage, by the same ids.
- **Notices:** the guessed "games are over" notice is gone (its list cleared
  once at sign-in, `clearGuessedNotices`); the Worker sends 中獎 NT$… /
  投注單沒中 (`push.js notifyNow`), tag `odds:slip:<id>`.
- **Sources in the Worker:** the Asian leagues' months and F1's archive are

### Stage 2: the Worker settles (the design as first written)
The design proposed (the owner asked to think it through, keep it simple):
- **Index by game, not slip.** Firestore (not KV: KV's free tier is 1,000
  writes a day, TRUTH §7), e.g. `odds-games/<gameKey>`: `{ due, legs-to-read,
  slips: ['<account>/<slipId>'] }` and `odds-open/<account>_<slipId>`:
  `{ account, slip spec (legs, sizes, stake, boost, free, freeValue, cost),
  games: [keys] }`. `gameKey` = the source and id (`espn:<path>:<date>:<away>@<home>`,
  `kambi:<id>`, `asia:<league>:<start>:<home>`, `f1:<kind>:<start>`,
  `pm:<slug>`).
- **Registered when a bet is placed** (and every open slip once, on the
  first open after deploy): a small authenticated call (with the session,
  like `/push`), from `placeSlip`/`placeFreeSlip`.
- **Cron** (the existing 5-minute schedule in `worker.js`): games with
  `due <= now` (first due = expected end: start + `GAME_HOURS` + margin),
  a few per run (CPU, below) → `readOutcomes` with `read = fetch` (the
  Worker reads sources directly; Kambi's kept copies from `kambi.js`'s own
  store) → for every slip on a decided game: `legResult` all its legs with
  the stored outcomes of its games; when settled (`applyResults` logic /
  `settleSlip`): add `payout-<slip>` (and `plus-<slip>`) to the wallet
  (`mergeWallet`, fixed ids: never twice), keep `{ slipId, results, finals,
  payout, at }` for Play to apply, send the real won/lost notice
  (`push.js sendPush`, if subscribed), delete the slip's open doc. A game
  not final: `due = now + 15 min`; void rules as today (3 days; Kambi
  `kambiUnresolvable`).
- **Cash-out**: the phone tells the Worker to drop the slip; if the Worker
  settled it already, its result stands.
- **CPU**: Cloudflare's free plan: 10 ms CPU an invocation, cron included.
  A 300–900 KB ESPN summary (props, next-run, corners) costs a few ms to
  parse. Settle a game or two per invocation and fan out (a fetch to the
  Worker itself is a new invocation), or read the day's scoreboard (one
  per league-day covers many games) and the summary only for prop picks.
- **Tests**: the phone and the Worker settling the same slip (never paid
  twice), cash-out racing settlement, a game postponed, Kambi dropping a
  match, a parlay with a lost pick settling before its other games.

### The owner's decision (2026-10-12, later): lock the money down instead

Measured first: pricing one game takes 6 ms of CPU (MLB, its score grids),
2 ms (soccer), 0.3 ms (NBA); F1's extra markets 94 ms (`f1Markets`'s drawn
races). On the free plan's 10 ms an invocation a whole board can't be priced
in the background, so the owner chose: **the phone keeps pricing; the proxy
checks**. Then, told that the pass accepts any money entry an app sends
(`eco.js cleanEntry`: any amount up to NT$1e9 from an app session, and
Securities' `snap.stock.cash` counted in the pool as sent), the owner chose
the widest lock-down: **Play and Securities, every money path checked by
the proxy, which becomes the only one adding money.** The plan, in stages
(each pushed on its own, nothing broken between):

1. Play's pricing into the kit (`kit/play/…`: sources' game building with
   the reader passed in, board, lines, house, markets, offers, propmodel,
   live), Play re-exporting, so the Worker prices with the same code.
2. Bets placed through the Worker (`/play op: 'place'`): each pick priced
   again by the Worker (one invocation a pick when needed: CPU), the slip
   refused if a price is better than the Worker's ("a price that moved
   stops it and says so"); registered with the server's time (before every
   pick's start). Live picks priced from the live game; F1's extras within
   a tolerance (drawn races).
3. Cash out: the value from the Worker's own prices (`cashout.mjs`).
4. Lottery: tickets bought through the Worker (before the close), settled
   and paid by it from Taiwan Lottery's draws (house games from BINGO's).
   Scratch cards drawn by the Worker at purchase.
5. The pass: an app's positive entries refused (`cleanEntry`: stakes and
   tickets only, ≤ 0); every payout, cash out, refund, prize the Worker's.
6. Securities: orders filled by the Worker at its own quote, the account's
   events verified and its cash figure computed by the Worker (`replay` in
   the kit), not taken from the phone's `snap`.

**All six stages are done and live (2026-10-12, 16:00-20:10 Taiwan).**
- 1: Play's pricing in the kit (`kit/play-*.mjs`: sources with `useReader`,
  bets, board, lines, live, house, kambi, offers, players, propmodel,
  schedules, teams, cashout, lottery, scratch); Play's files re-export them.
  One-league loaders (`loadLeague`, `loadF1Race`, `loadFuture`) equal the

- 2: `/play op 'place'` (settle.js + price.js): every pick priced again by

  price). Dev door: `POST /play { op: 'check' }` from localhost.
- 3: cash out (`op 'cashout', value`) priced by the Worker (cashOutQuote),
  paid by it; equal to Play's own value on live data.
- 4: lottery (`lotto.js`): tickets sold by the Worker's clock, settled from

  by it; older tickets handed over once (`tickets-open`).
- 5: the pass (`eco.js appEntries`): an app's entries are spending only (one

  only Securities' Worker-counted cash counts in the pool.
- 6: Securities (`stock-check.js`): its account replayed by the Worker with

- LOCK_SINCE / PLACE_SINCE = 2026-10-12T09:00Z: slips, tickets and
  Securities events from before are taken as they were.
- Measured: a free-plan Worker in practice isn't cut at 10 ms (the sports
  proxy parses Polymarket's 11.7 MB uncached and answers): the price check

- **Not seen end to end on a real pass** (Claude never signs in): the

### The owner's last call: the whole odds calculation on the proxy (superseded above)
"Move the whole odd calculation to proxy, so Quadra app itself only
receive/request and show live stats." Not started. **Decide first, with
the owner:**
- **CPU.** Pricing a board is heavy: ESPN + Polymarket + Kambi reads,
  de-vigging, the house model (`house.mjs`: strengths from standings),
  score grids (`play-odds.mjs scoreGrid`, `fitTeamRuns`), props
  (`propmodel.mjs`, `props.mjs`), F1 and futures, the lottery's cut
  (`play-rules.mjs`). On the free plan's 10 ms per invocation that's not
  realistic per request. Options: (a) Workers Paid ($5/month, 30 s CPU;
  TRUTH says free tiers, so it's the owner's call); (b) boards priced in
  the background (cron, a league at a time, each a separate invocation)
  and stored (KV is write-limited; Firestore or the Cache API), the app
  reading the stored board; (c) a mix: the static board in the
  background, only live re-pricing (cash-out) on request.
- **What moves**: `sources.mjs` board building (`mergeGames`,
  `withHousePrices`, Kambi boards, Polymarket, futures, F1 pricing),
  `board.mjs`, `offers.mjs`, `propmodel.mjs`, `house.mjs`, `cashout.mjs`
  (live cash-out value), `play-odds.mjs`, `play-rules.mjs`. Stage 1's kit
  modules are the start; the rest are app-side today.
- **What stays on the phone**: showing the board, the slip builder,
  placing bets (the account and wallet writes), live stats display.
- **Bets must be priced by the server too**, or a phone could send any
  odds: placing a bet should be checked (or done) by the Worker against
  its own board. That's the real safety gain of the move.
